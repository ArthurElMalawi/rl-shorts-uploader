Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Data used

The app only accesses the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts of its owner, after the owner explicitly authorizes it (OAuth). Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app keeps a local log of published videos (file name, title, post ID, date). No data from any other user is collected, accessed or stored.

2. TikTok data

From TikTok, the app reads only basic account information (user.info.basic) to confirm which account is connected, and the creator information needed to choose an allowed privacy level before posting. It uses video.upload and video.publish only to post the owner's own videos.

3. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties. The only connections are between the owner's computer and YouTube/Google, Instagram/Meta and TikTok.

4. Third-party services

Those services' own policies apply: Google (including the YouTube Terms), Instagram, TikTok.

5. Deletion and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time: Google account permissions, Instagram settings (Apps and websites), TikTok settings (Security › Apps and services). The local tokens and logs can be deleted at any time.
