RL Shorts Uploader

Rocket League Shorts from the Azuzti channel, published once a day to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. One video, three platforms, through the official APIs only.

Latest Shorts. Each one is edited from the owner's own Rocket League matches: goals, saves, mechanics and funny moments, cut to music.

How it works

From a recorded match to a published Short, in four steps.

  1. Record and edit

    Highlights are clipped from the owner's own matches and edited into vertical 9:16 Shorts with music, effects and captions.

  2. Quality check

    Every video is checked automatically before release: format, duration, audio levels, title and hashtags.

  3. Daily queue

    Videos are released in weekly batches of 7. The queue publishes one Short per day.

  4. Publish everywhere

    The same video is uploaded to YouTube (Data API v3), Instagram (Instagram API) and TikTok (Content Posting API), with the same title on each.

TikTok integration

How the app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

StepWhat happens
Sign inThe account owner signs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions. The access token is stored on the owner's computer only.
Scopesuser.info.basic to show which account is connected, video.upload and video.publish to post the owner's own videos.
Before postingCreator info is queried so that only the privacy levels allowed for the account are offered. Comments, Duet and Stitch stay enabled.
PostingThe video file and its title are sent to TikTok. The resulting post ID is saved in a local log.
Revoking accessAccess can be removed at any time in TikTok: Settings › Security › Apps and services.

FAQ

Who can use RL Shorts Uploader?

Only the owner of the Azuzti channel, to publish their own videos to their own accounts. It is not sold or distributed, and it has no public sign-up.

What data does it collect?

None from other people. It stores the owner's authorization tokens and a log of published videos (file name, title, post ID, date) on the owner's computer. See the Privacy Policy.

What content is published?

Only Rocket League videos edited by the owner from their own gameplay. See the Terms of Service.
